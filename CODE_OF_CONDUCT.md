# Code of conduct

This project is a small community effort. Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to:

- Be respectful and patient, including with people who are new, who write in another language, or who disagree.
- Discuss data and methods, not people. Criticism of the model is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Not post harassment, discriminatory language, sexual content, or other people's private information.
- Not make unsourced accusations against the utility, its staff or any other organisation or person.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block participants who do not follow these rules.

To report a problem privately, use the same route as for security reports in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and say that it concerns conduct.
